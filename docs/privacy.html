Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

← Back to Clutch

In short: Clutch reads your screenshots, photos and text on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, we run no servers, and we collect no data about you. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. Who we are

Clutch is an iOS app by Ernist Isabekov that turns screenshots, photos and text into tasks and reminders. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. Questions: support@clutch.app.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data. Clutch has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services of its own.

3. How your screenshots are read

  • Text is read from the image with Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision).
  • Tasks, dates and places are found on your iPhone: by Apple Intelligence on iPhones that support it and have it turned on (Apple's on-device model), otherwise by Clutch's own built-in reader.
  • Your screenshots, photos and text are never sent to us or to any AI service. Clutch works offline.
  • Your screenshots stay in your Photos library. Clutch only reads the ones you choose.

Nothing leaves your iPhone. Older versions of Clutch (before version 2.1) sent screenshot text to an AI service to find tasks. Current versions do not.

4. Data on your device

  • Extracted tasks and app settings are stored on your iPhone.
  • Your free-extraction count and Pro status are kept in your device's Keychain.
  • Tasks you save go to Apple Reminders or Calendar, using Apple's on-device APIs. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs them across your devices under Apple's privacy policy.
  • For a location reminder, the place name (for example "Supermarket" or a café) is looked up with Apple's geocoding service, and the reminder is handled by Apple Reminders. Your own location is used only on your iPhone to trigger those reminders and is never sent to us.
  • Exports (CSV, Todoist, Notion) are files you create and share yourself.

5. Apple services

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide us anonymous, aggregated reports under Apple's own policy.

6. Permissions

  • Photos: to read the screenshots and photos you pick.
  • Camera: only if you take a photo in Clutch.
  • Reminders and Calendar: to save the tasks you choose (Calendar access is add-only).
  • Location: only for reminders that go off when you arrive at or leave a place.

You can change any of these in Settings on your iPhone at any time.

7. Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you are in full control. Use "Clear data" in Clutch's settings or delete the app. Reminders and calendar events you saved stay in Apple's apps until you delete them there.

8. Children

Clutch collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
