FAQ

Questions, answered

What counts as an extraction?

An extraction counts only when Clutch successfully finds at least one task, whether from a screenshot, a photo or pasted text. If nothing is found, or something goes wrong, it doesn't count. Free users also tap "Extract Tasks" first, so a free extraction is never used by accident.

Do I need a screenshot?

No. Tap the text button to paste an email, message or note, or share text to Clutch from Mail, Notes, Messages and other apps.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up or login. Download the app, allow access to your photos, and start extracting.

Where do my tasks go?

Tasks you select are saved to Apple Reminders with due dates, alerts and priority, so they sync to your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch through iCloud. Pro users can also add them to Calendar as events, or export to CSV, Todoist and Notion.

Does Clutch read my calendar?

No. Clutch only asks for permission to add events. It can't see the events already in your calendar.

Which languages does Clutch understand?

Clutch works best with English, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Chinese text.

What happens to my screenshots?

Clutch only processes the screenshots and text you choose to extract. To find tasks, their content is sent to our AI service. Your screenshots stay in your Photos library. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Clutch. You can also tap "Manage Subscription" in Clutch's settings. You keep Pro until the end of the current period.

Which devices are supported?

Clutch is available for iPhone running iOS 26.2 or later.
